An Introduction

The Busy Dad Program

The unbelievably simple, effective, and time-efficient training protocol I've been following ever since my daughter was born. It's gotten me into the best shape of my life, and it can do the same for you.

  • No matter your fitness level, if you can find just 80 minutes per week and pledge to fill those minutes with intentional physical training, BDP can work its magic on you the same way it has on me.

  • This site gives you everything you need to start. You'll learn the philosophy, the four-tiered level system, and the two Sacred Movements.

  • The journey starts here. Whoever you are — a busy dad, or a busy person of any kind — welcome aboard.

The Coach

Max Edwards

Founder of Busy Dad Training. Since the day my daughter was born I've been running on a strict training budget: two compound movements, four 20-minute sessions a week, and not a single wasted minute.

I built BDP for everyone who wants to get strong without surrendering their evenings to a gym. If you're a busy dad — or just a busy person — the program will meet you where you are.
